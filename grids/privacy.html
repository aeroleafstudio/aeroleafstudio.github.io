Grids Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Grids does not collect any information about you. It works offline: it has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Who makes Grids

Grids is a puzzle game for Android made by Aeroleaf, an independent developer. You can reach us at contact.aeroleaf@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Grids has no sign-in and no accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for access to the internet, so it cannot send anything about you or your device anywhere.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, Grids keeps a few things on your phone or tablet:

  • the puzzles you have started, with your moves, so you can resume and undo them;
  • the size and difficulty you chose for your next puzzles, and which game you played last;
  • your day streak and your best streak;
  • a history of the puzzles you have solved (the puzzle, its game, size and difficulty, your time, the hints you used and when you solved it), which the Stats screen shows.

This information never leaves your device through Grids, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling Grids, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Device backups

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this information in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so it comes back when you set up a new device. Those backups are made and stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not have access to them. You can turn backup off in your device's settings.

Permissions

Grids does not ask for any permissions: not your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other data.

Children

Grids does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If Grids ever changes how it handles information (for example, if it begins to show ads), this policy will be updated before that version of the app is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Grids: contact.aeroleaf@gmail.com.
